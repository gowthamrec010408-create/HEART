CARDIAC WARNING & DIAGNOSTIC RULES ENGINE

Validated Multi-Parameter Clinical Threshold Monitoring

HEART RATE THRESHOLDS

  • Critical High: > 130 BPM (Severe Tachycardia)
  • Warning High: 101 - 120 BPM
  • Normal Range: 60 - 100 BPM
  • Warning Low: 50 - 59 BPM
  • Critical Low: < 40 BPM (Severe Bradycardia)

OXYGEN SATURATION (SpO2)

  • Normal Range: 95% - 100%
  • Hypoxia Warning: 91% - 94%
  • Critical Hypoxemia: ≤ 90%

ECG SIGNAL INTEGRITY

  • Lead Off (LO+/LO-): Triggers "SENSOR DISCONNECTED"
  • High Noise: Triggers "WARNING: Poor Signal"
  • Safety Rule: Lead disconnection does NOT trigger false cardiac emergencies.
IMPORTANT MEDICAL & TECHNICAL DISCLAIMER: This system is an IoT hardware prototype developed for educational, experimental, and remote monitoring demonstration. Single-lead AD8232 ECG and optical MAX30102 sensors are not certified diagnostic medical equipment and cannot confirm or diagnose myocardial infarction (heart attack) or complex cardiac conditions.
